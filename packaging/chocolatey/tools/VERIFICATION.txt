To verify:

1. Download the matching archive from the release this package was built from:

     https://github.com/Janne6565/wharf-tui/releases

     Get-FileHash wharf_<version>_windows_amd64.zip -Algorithm SHA256

     Get-FileHash .\wharf.exe -Algorithm SHA256

   The hashes must match.
